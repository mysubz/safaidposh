Put your own hero videos here (MP4, H.264, ideally under 8 MB each, muted).

Then, in index.html, set the data-video attribute on a hero slide, for example:

  <div class="slide" data-video="videos/hero-1.mp4"> ... </div>

The slide's image stays as the fallback if the video can't load.
Only use footage you own or have a licence for.
